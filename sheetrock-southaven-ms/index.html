Southaven, MS

Drywall work built for how Southaven builds.

DeSoto County builds at a different pace than the rest of North Mississippi. Southaven, Olive Branch, and Horn Lake carry the volume of a metro suburb — subdivision construction, larger commercial and retail space, and a constant flow of remodel work on homes built through the county's growth years.

Pulliam Sheetrock Service works this market for both builders and homeowners. New construction gets hung and finished on schedule; existing homes get repair and remodel work that matches the surrounding finish instead of standing out from it.
