Oxford, MS

Drywall work built for how Oxford builds.

Oxford runs on a building calendar most towns do not have. Between the University of Mississippi rental market, the condo and townhome inventory around campus, and steady new construction out toward the bypass, there is a real premium on drywall work that can be scheduled tightly and finished clean the first time.

Pulliam Sheetrock Service handles both sides of that market. For builders and remodelers we hang and finish whole interiors. For landlords and property managers we do the unglamorous work that actually matters between leases — patched holes, repaired corners, and re-finished walls that make a unit show well on a short window.
