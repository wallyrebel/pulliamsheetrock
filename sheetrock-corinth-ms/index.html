Corinth, MS

Drywall work built for how Corinth builds.

Corinth sits on a mix of building stock that not many North Mississippi towns share. There are century-old homes near the historic district with plaster and settled framing, mid-century houses on the north and west sides, and steady new construction pushing out along the Highway 72 corridor. Each one asks something different from a sheetrock crew.

Pulliam Sheetrock Service works across all of it. On new builds we hang, tape, and finish whole houses so the painter can walk in behind us. On older Corinth homes we take on the harder problems — tying new rock into old plaster walls, chasing settling cracks that keep coming back, and rebuilding ceilings after a roof leak.

Questions

Sheetrock questions from Corinth customers.

Do you work in Corinth and the rest of Alcorn County?

Yes. Corinth is one of our core service areas, and we cover the surrounding Alcorn County communities including Rienzi, Glen, Farmington, Kossuth, and Biggersville.

Can you match the finish on an older Corinth home?

Yes. Matching an existing wall is mostly about surface texture and feathering the joint far enough out. On older homes with plaster or heavy texture we match the surrounding surface so the repair disappears under paint.

Do you take small repair jobs in Corinth, or only full builds?

Both. A single patched ceiling is as welcome as a full house. No job is too large or too small.
