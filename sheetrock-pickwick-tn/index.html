Pickwick, TN

Drywall work built for how Pickwick builds.

The Pickwick Lake area builds differently from inland North Mississippi. There are lake houses with vaulted great rooms and long uninterrupted wall runs, cabins with tricky angles, and a large stock of seasonal and short-term rental property that takes hard use and needs regular repair.

Pickwick sits a short drive from our Mississippi service area, and we cover it regularly. The work runs from full interiors on new lake construction to the humidity and leak repairs that come with property near the water.
